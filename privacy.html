Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains how the Emotion Diary mobile app handles information. It applies to the app, not to unrelated third-party websites or services.

1. Diary data and device storage

Emotion Diary is a local-first app. Diary entries, moods, notes, profile information, and app settings are stored on your device. The app does not send your diary content to servers operated by Emotion Diary and does not provide accounts or cloud backup services.

In the current release, diary and profile data are encrypted before being stored in the app's local storage. The encryption key and an optional app PIN are stored separately using the secure storage provided by the operating system (Android Keystore or Apple Keychain).

2. Advertising and privacy choices

The app may use Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device, network, and advertising-identifier information to deliver, measure, limit the frequency of, and protect advertising, as described in Google's policies.

Before requesting ads, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to update consent information and to show a consent form where required. Ads are not requested unless the consent SDK reports that ads may be requested. The app's Settings screen includes an Advertising Privacy Settings entry point so that eligible users can review or change their choices.

The current release does not include Firebase Analytics.

3. Retention and deletion

Emotion Diary does not retain a copy of your diary content on its servers, so we cannot access, recover, or remotely delete it. You can remove locally stored app data through your device's system settings or by uninstalling the app. This may permanently remove your local diary data. Any backup, restoration, or secure-storage behavior is controlled by your device and operating-system settings.

5. Security

We use encryption and operating-system secure storage to reduce the risk of unauthorized access to local diary data. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The current version and its update date will be published on this page.
